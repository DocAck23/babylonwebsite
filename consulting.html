Advisory Practices

Two practices. The same standard of execution.

Beyond our turbine work, Babylon Consulting maintains two focused advisory practices—medical practice consulting and AI & data consulting—where operational results, not slide decks, are the deliverable.

Practice 01

Medical Practice Consulting

We help medical and specialty practices run better. Our work spans operations, growth, and the systems that let clinicians spend their time on patients rather than administration.

Engagements are practical and hands-on: we diagnose where time, revenue, and patient experience leak, then implement the fixes—staffing models, workflow redesign, scheduling, intake, and the technology layer underneath it all.

i.

Operations & Workflow

Scheduling, intake, documentation, and patient flow redesigned to reduce friction and reclaim clinician time.

ii.

Growth & Patient Experience

Practice growth, retention, and a patient experience that earns referrals—built on systems, not guesswork.

iii.

Systems & Technology

Selecting and implementing the practice-management and digital tools that actually fit how the practice works.

Practice 02

AI & Data Consulting

For organizations modernizing how they operate, we provide AI strategy, workflow automation, and data architecture—translated into systems that run in production, not pilots that stall.

We start from the work, not the tool: where does judgment get repeated, where does data sit unused, where does a process beg for automation? Then we build, integrate, and hand over something the team can actually rely on.

i.

AI Strategy

A clear-eyed assessment of where AI creates real leverage—and where it does not—mapped to your operations and budget.

ii.

Workflow Automation

Automating the repetitive, rules-based work that drains teams, with humans kept firmly in the loop where it matters.

iii.

Data Architecture

The data foundation—clean, connected, and governed—that any serious AI or analytics effort depends on.
