How We Transact

Principal to principal. Every time.

Our process is built for clarity of title and certainty of delivery.

i.

We Buy and Sell on Our Own Account

Babylon Consulting transacts as a direct principal. When you buy from us, you are buying from the seller—not through a chain of go-betweens.

ii.

Verified Condition & Documentation

Every unit we sell is supported by specifications, running-hour records, and provenance. We represent assets accurately, and we put it in writing.

iii.

Clear Title & Terms

Purchase and sale agreements with unambiguous title, pricing, and delivery terms—structured so both sides know exactly where they stand.

iv.

Logistics & Delivery

We coordinate heavy-lift logistics, shipping, and handover, and arrange commissioning support where it is needed.
