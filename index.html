The Practice

Heavy rotating equipment is a market of relationships.

A steam or gas turbine is among the most valuable, most scrutinized assets a power project will ever buy. Availability is opaque, provenance matters, lead times are long, and the wrong unit is an expensive mistake measured in years.

Babylon Consulting works in this market as a direct principal—acquiring and selling turbine units, verifying condition and documentation, and standing behind the transaction from agreement to delivery. We hold the relationships, the technical fluency, and the discretion the work demands.

How a Turbine Transaction Works
