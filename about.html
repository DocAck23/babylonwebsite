A principal in the market—not a name on the sidelines.

Who We Are

Focused by design.

Babylon Consulting LLC is a private firm headquartered in the United States and active worldwide. Our core business is the acquisition and sale of steam and gas turbines—we transact as a direct principal, holding the relationships and technical fluency that heavy rotating equipment demands.

Around that core, we run two advisory practices we believe in: medical practice consulting and AI & data consulting. We keep our work narrow on purpose. We would rather be excellent at a few things than adequate at many.

Principles

How we work.

i.

We Act as a Principal

In our turbine business we buy and sell on our own account. Buyers and sellers deal with us directly, with clear title and clear terms.

ii.

Accuracy Over Salesmanship

We represent every asset and every engagement as it truly is—condition, documentation, scope, and timeline—in writing.

iii.

Discretion as Default

The details of our transactions and engagements stay private. Parties are named only by consent.

iv.

Operational, Not Theoretical

We are measured by outcomes—units delivered, practices improved, systems running—never by the length of a report.

v.

Compliance First

We work within applicable laws and sanctions, and we decline anything that presents unacceptable legal, ethical, or reputational risk.

vi.

Selective by Choice

We take on a small number of engagements at a time so that each gets the attention it deserves.

Introduce Yourself

The best work starts with a conversation.

Tell us who you are and what you are trying to accomplish—buying or selling a turbine, or engaging one of our advisory practices. We respond to serious inquiries within two business days.
